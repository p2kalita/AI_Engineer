The global semiconductor shortage that began in 2020 has reshaped how companies think about supply chain resilience. What started as a temporary disruption caused by pandemic-related factory shutdowns quickly became a structural challenge affecting industries from automotive to consumer electronics. Major automakers lost an estimated $210 billion in revenue during 2021 alone. The response has been massive investment in domestic chip manufacturing, with the U.S. CHIPS Act allocating $52 billion in subsidies.
